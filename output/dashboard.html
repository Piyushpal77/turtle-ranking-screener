📐 Turtle Trend Following & 5-Pillar Multi-Indicator Grading Architecture

Pillar 1: Breakout & ATR Structure 25% Weight

  • ATR-Normalized Breakout Impulse (Breakout_N): Measures |Close - Donchian_Level| / N. Rewards decisive 0.35N – 1.50N closes beyond the channel while tapering above 2.5N to avoid chasing overextended overnight earnings gaps.
  • Base Volatility Compression (N20 / SMA60(TR)): Rewards breakouts emerging from low-volatility accumulation bases.
  • System 2 Priority Bonus: 55-day Donchian breakouts receive priority and structural weighting over 20-day breakouts.

Pillar 2: Multi-Horizon Momentum & MACD 25% Weight

  • Volatility-Adjusted 60d Momentum: ±ROC_60 / ATR% measures intermediate risk-adjusted trend velocity.
  • N-Normalized MACD (12, 26, 9) Histogram: ±MACD_Hist / N plus 3-day histogram acceleration confirms fresh momentum inflection on the breakout bar.

Pillar 3: Hull MA (20) & Macro Regime 20% Weight

  • Hull Moving Average (HMA_20): Zero-lag weighted moving average curve WMA(√20, 2·WMA(10) - WMA(20)).
  • 5-Day HMA N-Slope: ±(HMA_20[t] - HMA_20[t-5]) / N confirms fast structural trend slope.
  • 200-Day SMA Alignment: Rewards breakouts aligned with the long-term institutional trend regime.

Pillar 4: Oscillator Power-Zone (RSI & Stoch) 15% Weight

  • Trend-Following RSI(14) Curve: Unlike mean-reversion screens, successful Day-1 Turtle breakouts occur in the bullish power zone (RSI 56–76 Long / 24–44 Short). Scores peak at 67 (Long) / 33 (Short) and penalize extreme blow-off exhaustion (>82 Long / <18 Short).
  • Slow Stochastic (14, 3, 3): Combines directional %K positioning and positive %K - %D momentum spread.

Pillar 5: Institutional Volume & ADX 15% Weight

  • Relative Volume Surge (RVOL): Volume[t] / SMA_20(Volume[t-1]) verifies institutional participation on breakout day.
  • Wilder's ADX(14) & DI Spread: Combines ADX_14 trend strength with directional dominance ±(+DI_14 - -DI_14).

Lifecycle Tracking & Freeze Rules Zero Look-Ahead

  • Fresh Day-1 Trigger: Qualifies only on Day 1 when Close crosses the prior 20d/55d Donchian channel.
  • Daily Chart Annotations: Every chart displays a vertical dotted line on the recommendation date and a horizontal dashed line at the recommended price.
  • Freeze on Turtle Exit: Updates daily until Close breaches the System-matched opposite channel (10-day Low/High for System 1; 20-day Low/High for System 2), at which point the row and chart freeze as EXITED.
